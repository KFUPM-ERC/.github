## About us

We are the KFUPM rover team, working toward participation in the **European Rover Challenge (ERC)**. We bring together mechanical design, electronics, software, and science to develop a rover and the systems needed to operate it.

Our goal is to turn engineering ideas into a reliable, integrated robot through design, prototyping, and field testing.

## Our engineering focus

- **Mechanical systems** — chassis, mobility, robotic manipulation, and payload integration.
- **Electronics & embedded systems** — power distribution, motor control, sensors, and onboard interfaces.
- **Autonomy & perception** — localization, mapping, navigation, and environmental understanding.
- **Mission control & communications** — operator interfaces, telemetry, camera feeds, and rover connectivity.
- **Science & testing** — scientific payloads, experiment workflows, simulation, and system validation.

## From prototype to field

A rover succeeds when its subsystems work together. Our development approach centers on clear interfaces, repeatable tests, and feedback from real operation. We aim to document what we build so teammates can reproduce results and keep improving the system.

This organization is the home for our rover development work. Browse the repositories for available code, documentation, and project-specific setup instructions.
